01 · Mandate

Independent statistics for public use

The Vantara National Statistical Office (NSO) is the country's central statistical authority. It was established by the Statistics Act 1998 and reports to Parliament through the National Statistics Council. The office compiles, analyses and publishes official statistics on the population, economy, society and environment.

The NSO publishes without prior clearance from ministers or other public bodies. Its professional independence, methodological choices and release dates are protected by law. The 2026 release covers the reference year 2025 and draws on the 2021 Population and Housing Census, continuing surveys and administrative records from other public institutions.

All figures are national estimates unless a page says otherwise. Six statistical regions are used for subnational data: Velhara, Marren, Ostria, Kaleth, Surna and Belen.

02 · Methodology

How the figures are produced

Population and migration

Population estimates use the cohort-component method. The base is the 2021 Census, adjusted for net undercount. Each year, births and deaths from the civil registration system are added or subtracted by age and sex, and net migration is estimated from border, residence-permit and survey data. The 2025 estimate is provisional until the post-enumeration check of the 2026 population register is complete.

National accounts

Gross domestic product is compiled using the production approach, consistent with the System of National Accounts 2008. Current-price estimates are built up from enterprise surveys, government finance statistics and external trade records. Volume estimates are chain-linked at the previous year's prices. Quarterly GDP is annualised only where the page says so; the annual series is the sum of the four quarters.

Prices

The consumer price index is a Laspeyres index using the 2021 Household Expenditure Survey basket. Prices are collected monthly in 18 urban centres and 12 rural districts, covering 412 goods and services. The annual inflation rate is the average of the twelve monthly indices compared with the previous year's average.

Labour market

The Labour Force Survey is a quarterly household survey with a rotating sample of about 4,800 households. It covers people aged 15 and over in private households. Unemployment, employment and participation follow International Labour Organization definitions. Annual figures are simple averages of the four quarters and are not seasonally adjusted.

Health and education

Health indicators combine vital registration, Ministry of Health service records and the annual health facility census. Education indicators combine the school census, higher-education enrolment records and the literacy module of the Labour Force Survey. Both series are checked against the previous year's returns before publication.

03 · Definitions

Terms used in this release

Nominal GDP
The market value of all goods and services produced in Vantara in a year, at that year's prices, in US dollars converted at the average official exchange rate.
Real GDP growth
The annual change in GDP measured at constant prices, chain-linked to the previous year. It removes the effect of price changes.
GDP per capita
Nominal GDP divided by the population at 31 December of the same year. It is a measure of average output, not of household income.
Consumer price inflation
The annual percentage change in the consumer price index, an average of the twelve monthly indices against the previous year's average.
Unemployment rate
The number of people without work, available to start within two weeks and actively seeking work in the previous four weeks, as a share of the labour force.
Labour force participation rate
The labour force — employed plus unemployed people — as a share of the population aged 15 to 64.
Life expectancy at birth
The average number of years a newborn would live if current age-specific mortality rates stayed unchanged throughout their life.
Adult literacy rate
The share of people aged 15 and over who can read and write a short, simple statement about everyday life.
Net enrolment rate
The share of children of official school age for a level who are enrolled at that level. Gross enrolment counts all students, whatever their age.
Age dependency ratio
People aged under 15 and 65 and over, expressed per 100 people aged 15 to 64.

Estimates are rounded to the precision shown. Components may not add exactly to totals because of rounding. Rates based on small samples carry wider confidence intervals than the published point estimates; the NSO publishes standard errors in the underlying tables.

Statistics are checked against administrative totals, previous returns and international classifications before release. Where a series is revised, the revision is documented in the release note and the full historical series is reissued.

05 · Revisions

A clear revision policy

Provisional estimates are labelled as such. Routine revisions are made when fuller source data become available, normally within 12 months. Methodological revisions are announced at least one quarter in advance and are accompanied by a back series. The 2025 population estimate is provisional; the 2026 release will incorporate the population register check.

Users who need a consistent historical series should use the latest release, which supersedes earlier publications. The NSO does not withdraw a published statistic without a public explanation.
