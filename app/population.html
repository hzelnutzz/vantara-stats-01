Population and migration

Population

Vantara's population reached 34.2 million at the end of 2025, 0.6% more than a year earlier. Growth is increasingly concentrated at older ages, and women outnumber men by 1.0 million.

Reading the figures

A slower, older population

Vantara added 200,000 people in 2025. Natural increase contributed 134,700 and net migration 65,300. The total fertility rate of 1.78 is below the replacement level of about 2.1, so migration now accounts for roughly a third of growth.

The median age is 34.6 years, up from 32.1 in 2016. People aged 65 and over make up 12.4% of the population, while children under 15 make up 21.3%. The age dependency ratio is 50.8 dependants per 100 people of working age.

Just over two-thirds of Vantarans live in urban areas, concentrated in the capital region around Velhara and the coastal corridor.
