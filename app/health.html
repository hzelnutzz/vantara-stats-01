Health and mortality

Health

Mortality

Gains concentrated at older ages

Life expectancy rose 3.0 years between 2016 and 2025. Most of the gain comes from lower mortality from circulatory disease and road injuries among people aged 50 and over. The gap between women and men narrowed slightly, from 5.1 years in 2016 to 5.6 years in 2025, as male mortality fell faster.

Infant mortality fell from 16.9 to 10.8 per 1,000 live births, and under-five mortality from 20.4 to 13.1. Maternal mortality nearly halved, from 31 to 18 deaths per 100,000 live births.

Health system

More staff, wider access

Vantara had 3.4 physicians and 3.9 hospital beds per 1,000 people in 2025, up from 2.7 and 3.5 in 2016. Health spending reached 7.8% of GDP, or about $53.6 billion at current prices.

DTP3 immunisation covered 94% of one-year-olds, and 96.2% of the population used safely managed drinking water. Rural access improved fastest, although it remains below the national average.
