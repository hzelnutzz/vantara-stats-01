What the latest figures show

  • $687.4B

    Growth held close to its long-run rate

    Real GDP rose 2.8% in 2025. Services account for 61.1% of output, up from 57.0% a decade earlier, while agriculture's share fell to 6.4%.

  • 2.4%

    Inflation returned to its pre-2021 range

    Consumer prices rose 2.4% on average in 2025, down from 7.8% in 2022 and 3.1% in 2024. Food and energy prices slowed most.

  • 76.4 yrs

    Life expectancy gained half a year

    A girl born in 2025 can expect to live 79.1 years and a boy 73.5 years. Infant mortality fell to 10.8 per 1,000 live births.

  • 6.2%

    Unemployment reached its lowest rate in the series

    The 2025 average was 0.3 percentage points below 2024. Youth unemployment also fell, to 14.6%, though it remains more than twice the national rate.
