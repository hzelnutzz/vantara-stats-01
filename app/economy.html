National accounts and prices

Economy

Vantara's economy grew 2.8% in real terms in 2025, to a nominal gross domestic product of $687.4 billion. Services produced 61.1% of output and consumer inflation averaged 2.4%.

Composition

Services take a larger share

Services produced 61.1% of GDP in 2025, up from 57.0% in 2016. Wholesale and retail trade, transport, business services and tourism account for most of the increase. Industry fell from 30.2% to 27.8% over the same period, and agriculture from 8.1% to 6.4%.

Net taxes on products contributed 4.7% of GDP. In cash terms, services were worth about $420 billion of the $687.4 billion total.

Prices and public finances

Inflation settled near its target

Consumer prices rose 2.4% on average in 2025, well below the 7.8% recorded in 2022. Food price inflation slowed to 3.1% and energy prices were 0.8% lower than a year earlier. Core inflation, which excludes food and energy, was 2.1%.

General government gross debt stood at 51.8% of GDP, or about $356 billion. Exports of goods and services were 32.3% of GDP and imports 29.1%, leaving a trade surplus of 3.2% of GDP.
